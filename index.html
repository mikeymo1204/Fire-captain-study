How to attack each source

Company Officer (6th Ed.)

The backbone of the written test and the most heavily weighted source. Read a chapter, then run that domain's topic quiz the same tour. Re‑quiz any domain under 80%.

Strategy & Tactics (2nd Ed.)

Fire dynamics through tactics. Pair this with the ICS/NIMS material — the incident‑management chapter overlaps heavily with command questions.

HCFR Policies, SOGs & the CBA

This is the local, memorization‑heavy block — and the most testable. Section numbers and CBA articles get tested almost verbatim. The CBA — Contract Articles and HCFR Policies & Procedures topic drills now cover this material directly, including the exact Company Fire Captain promotion requirements and the written‑test format from Article 26.

Test format (Art. 26): written exam, first two weeks of October, drawn from a bank of 200+ questions, max 150 questions, 70% to pass. Questions missed by 50%+ of candidates are discarded and the rest rescaled to 100%.
